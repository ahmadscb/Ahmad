# ElevenLabs voiceover script: SlideSpoken 40 s ad

## Option A (recommended): seven clips, one per scene
Generate each line as its own clip, then place each clip at its start time in your editor. Every clip then lands on its scene, whatever pace the voice takes.

| # | Place at | Must end by | Paste this into ElevenLabs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.3 s | 3.9 s | What if your slides could present themselves... and answer questions? |
| 2 | 4.2 s | 8.8 s | Meet SlideSpoken. Upload your deck, or create one with AI. |
| 3 | 9.2 s | 15.8 s | An AI Presenter delivers it live. In the room, or on Google Meet, Zoom and Teams. |
| 4 | 16.2 s | 21.8 s | HR teams train new hires. Real estate agents pitch properties to investors. |
| 5 | 22.2 s | 27.8 s | Sales teams run product demos. Educators turn lessons into conversations. |
| 6 | 28.2 s | 33.8 s | Ask a question. SlideSpoken answers, then picks up right where it left off. |
| 7 | 34.2 s | 39.8 s | Stop just sharing slides. Let them speak. Start free at SlideSpoken dot com. |

If a clip runs past its "must end by" time, raise Speed to about 1.1 for that clip. Line 1 is the tightest, at 3.6 s.

## Option B: one continuous take
For Eleven Multilingual v2 or Turbo/Flash v2.5, which read `<break>` tags. Eleven v3 ignores them, so use Option A with v3. Start the audio at 0.3 s. The pauses are estimates; nudge a line in your editor if it drifts more than 0.3 s from its start time above.

```
What if your slides could present themselves... and answer questions? <break time="0.8s" />
Meet SlideSpoken. Upload your deck, or create one with AI. <break time="1.2s" />
An AI Presenter delivers it live. In the room, or on Google Meet, Zoom and Teams. <break time="1.0s" />
HR teams train new hires. Real estate agents pitch properties to investors. <break time="1.2s" />
Sales teams run product demos. Educators turn lessons into conversations. <break time="1.3s" />
Ask a question. SlideSpoken answers, then picks up right where it left off. <break time="0.9s" />
Stop just sharing slides. Let them speak. Start free at SlideSpoken dot com.
```

## Voice settings
- **Voice:** a confident, warm, conversational English voice.
- **Model:** Multilingual v2 for Option B.
- **Stability:** about 45%.
- **Similarity:** about 75%.
- **Style:** 10–20%.
- **Speed:** 1.0, or 1.05–1.1 if lines run long.
- **Pronunciation:** the script spells "SlideSpoken dot com" so the voice says the address correctly. "AI" and "HR" are read as letters.
- **Export:** WAV or MP3 at 44.1 kHz.
