The rendered MP4 carries a silent AAC track so you can lay in your own voice and music. Each line below is timed to its scene; the burned-in captions and `slidespoken-youtube-ad-40s.srt` use the same words and cue times. Start each line at its cue and land it before the next scene begins.

Voice direction: confident, warm, conversational, international English; about 150 words a minute. Leave a breath before "Meet SlideSpoken" (4.2 s) and before "Ask a question" (28.2 s): those are the two turns of the film.

Sound cues worth placing (visible actions): 0.2 s LIVE tag on; 1.2 s camera pull-back whoosh; 2.6 s question pop; 3.75 s whip out; 5.4 s file drop; 8.25 s click on In-Person Mode; 8.6 s zoom-through; 12.1 s screen-to-meeting push; 18.9 s slide whip; 28.5 s "question detected" chime; 32.6 s resume tick; 37.3 s CTA click; 39.0 s sonic logo.
