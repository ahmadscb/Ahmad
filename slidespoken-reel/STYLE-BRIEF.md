# SlideSpoken Reel: style brief (Bang Motion)

- **Product and feeling:** SlideSpoken, an AI agent that presents your slides. The viewer should come away feeling it is assured, alive and cinematic.
- **Concept candidates (opener-konsep.md):**
  1. #10 Question → answer inside the world: an audience question appears in the world and the product answers by acting.
  2. #5 Journey inside the UI: the camera flies through a giant live-presenter interface.
  3. #18 Through gradient shapes: the camera passes through glowing orbit rings into the logo.
- **Chosen: #10.** SlideSpoken's differentiator is that it *answers the audience*. That is most convincing when we see a question asked and answered in the world, not described.
- **Structure fingerprint:** `question → answer in the world · 5 acts · slide card presenting itself / slide close-up with the voice orb / question bubble + answer card blooming from the chart / Meet-Zoom-Teams window carousel around the orb / orb docking into the logo's waveform "o" · in media res (the deck is already presenting at frame 0) · logo born from the final object`
  - Different from the earlier SlideSpoken ad (hook → reveal → rooms → use cases → Q&A → end card).
- **Palette:**
  - Primary: lime `#d8ff73` (brand accent).
  - Background: `#060906` (derived: the brand's always-dark band `#111611`, deepened because the brief asks for cinematic dark).
  - Accent: olive `#5e7d22` / sage `#8a9b76` (brand accent-olive and accent-moss) for gradient depth.
- **Fonts:**
  - Display: Instrument Serif (regular + italic), for editorial cinema.
  - Labels: DM Mono, the brand's label font.
  - UI mockups: Inter, the product's own UI font.
- **Emphasis:** no highlight shapes. One italic word per line in lime (the brand's italic turn).
- **Background direction:** cinematic dark, grainy gradient. It holds for the whole piece, and the mood shifts through camera and light, not through colour cuts.
- **Motion signature:** the **voice orb**, a lime disc with live waveform bars (the "o" of the logo). It speaks, listens and travels between objects, and is the thread through the whole film. Headlines roll in word by word in 3D.
- **Background motion (§7c #6):** concentric brand orbit rings turning slowly in two parallax layers, a drifting lime glow, and live grain.
- **Background surface (§7d g):** grainy gradient, a deep green-black base with a lime/olive glow and noise grain.
- **Object render family (§7e):** glass and glowing orb; dark glass UI panels lit by lime.
- **Transitions:**
  - Camera push into the deck (motivated).
  - Rack-focus swing down to the question.
  - The answer card blooms out of the chart.
  - The deck shrinks while windows rotate in from depth (3D carousel).
  - The orb docks into the logo, followed by a circular reveal of the wordmark.
- **Special moment:** the orb flying into the logo and becoming its "o".
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 9:16, 25 s, 60 fps.
